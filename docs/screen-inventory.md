# FMS Screen Inventory

All screens must handle 5 states: Loading (Skeleton), Empty, Error (Boundary), Success, and Skeleton-on-filter.

| ID   | Name | Route | Allowed Roles | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S-01 | Login | `/login` | All | Single entry point. Displays generic auth errors. |
| S-01a | Set Password | `/set-password#token=…` | Public (one-time link) | Choose a first or new password from an invitation or reset link (FMS-78). The token stays in the fragment and is removed from the address bar. |
| S-04 | Vehicle Dashboard | `/vehicles` | Admin, Fleet Mgr, Dispatcher, Depot Admin | Data table with status badges and inline decommissioning. |
| S-05 | Driver Dashboard | `/drivers` | Admin, Fleet Mgr, Dispatcher, Depot Admin | Displays driver license status. Red 'EXPIRED' badges. |
| S-06 | Attendance UI | `/attendance` | Admin, Depot Admin, Fleet Mgr | Grid toggle (present/absent/on_leave) per driver. |
| S-07 | Dispatcher Trip Console | `/dispatch` | Admin, Fleet Mgr, Dispatcher (`trip:assign`) | Split view: the depot's trips from today (Unassigned and Assigned views) on the left; its drivers and vehicles on the right with their state now (drivers green available, amber finishing a trip, red on a trip, grey absent; vehicles green available, red flagged, grey on a trip) and whether they are free for the chosen trip; search by name, plate or model. Choosing a trip, a driver and a vehicle fills the assignment panel; a flagged vehicle shows its open work order there, and the API's refusals (BR-1 and BR-3 with the conflicting trip, BR-2, eligibility) and warnings (absent driver, overrun) stay inline in it. Refreshes every 30 s (FMS-33). |
| S-08 | Technician Repair Log | `/maintenance` | Admin, Tech | Form to log repairs, deduct stock, and clear maintenance flags. A repair records what failed (component, mode, unplanned) before it can be completed (FMS-125). |
| S-09 | Spare Parts Console | `/inventory` | Admin, Depot Admin, Tech | Live stock counts with 'OUT OF STOCK' / 'LOW' highlights. |
| S-10 | Audit Log UI | `/audit` | Admin, Fleet Owner, Compliance (`audit:read`) | Who changed what and when, newest first (FMS-29, [audit-log.md](audit-log.md)): views for data changes, sign-ins, denied requests, scheduled jobs and everything; find a record by its ID, filter by kind of record and period, and narrow to one person, record or request from an entry. An entry opens the fields it changed and only those, before and after, with money in ETB and fuel in litres. Read-only. |
| S-11 | Driver Mobile Home | `/driver` | Driver (`trip:execute`) | "My day" in large type: the trip under way, or the next one assigned today (times in Addis Ababa, from and to, the vehicle's plate, stops), other trips later today, and one large button: START TRIP (`POST /trips/{id}/start`), then END TRIP (`/end`), which leads to the post-trip inspection prompt (S-12). Refusals (flagged vehicle, expired licence) show under the button. Today only: no past trips. Works at 375px; with no signal it shows the day as last saved on the phone and START/END wait for signal (FMS-35). |
| S-12 | Driver DVIR Form | `/driver/dvir` (`?trip=&type=`) | Driver (`dvir:write`) | "Vehicle inspection": the pre- or post-trip checklist for the vehicle of the driver's own trip (from `?trip=`, else the trip under way or the next one today). Pre-trip: tyres, brakes, lights and indicators, windscreen and mirrors, horn, fuel level, engine oil, coolant, fire extinguisher, first aid kit; post-trip: tyres, brakes, lights and indicators, body damage, leaks, dashboard warning lights, fuel level, cabin. Each item needs OK or Problem, a problem takes an optional note (the defect detail), and any problem warns that the vehicle will be flagged. Submits `POST /dvir-reports` through the offline queue (FMS-81) with `submitted_at` and the trip: with no signal it is saved on the phone with the pending badge and sent when the signal returns. Reached from the Inspection tab, the next trip's "Pre-trip inspection" link and the post-trip prompt on S-11. Submit is disabled while 50 submissions wait (FMS-37). |
| S-13 | Driver Incident Form | `/driver/incident` (`?trip=`) | Driver (`incident:write`) | "Report an incident" from wherever the driver is: what happened (accident, breakdown, tyre, theft, vandalism, injury, road hazard, other; optional), how serious (Minor, Moderate, Serious, Critical, in plain words; Critical warns that dispatch is alerted and the vehicle must not be driven), a description, and optionally the phone's location. About the vehicle of the trip under way or the next one today; with no trip it goes without a vehicle. Submits `POST /incidents` with `occurred_at` (when it was reported) through the offline queue (FMS-81): with no signal it waits on the phone with the pending badge. The Incident tab (FMS-38, docs/incidents.md). |
| S-14 | Driver Fuel Entry | `/driver/fuel` (`?trip=`) | Driver (`fuel:write`) | "Log fuel" for the vehicle of the driver's own trip (from `?trip=`, else the trip under way or the next one today): litres (`FuelInput`), total paid (`MoneyInput`, with the price per litre shown), odometer (`OdometerInput`) and an optional station. Sent as whole numbers: 23.5 L is `quantity_ml: 23500`, ETB 2,890.50 is `total_cost_cents: 289050`; a negative amount is refused in the form. Submits `POST /fuel-logs` through the offline queue (FMS-81): with no signal it waits on the phone with the pending badge. The body carries `fueled_at`, the moment the driver pressed submit, so a fill-up sent hours later keeps its pump time (FMS-115). A refusal (odometer lower than the last reading) shows the server's reason and keeps the form. The Fuel tab (FMS-40). |
| S-15 | Fuel Reconciliation | `/fuel-reconciliation` | Admin, Fleet Manager, Finance Clerk, Compliance (vehicles and anomalies); Depot Admin (anomalies only) | A month's fuel logged per vehicle against what its trips needed, furthest off first (Flag beyond the threshold in red, Watch, OK, Not judged), with a summary band, CSV export and data freshness; the fuel anomalies, resolved or dismissed with who did it recorded (FMS-42, docs/fuel-reconciliation.md). |
| S-16 | Fleet Command Center | `/command-center` | Admin, Fleet Mgr, Dispatcher | 70% Live Map (Socket.io/Leaflet), 30% sidebar of active trips. |
| S-17 | Notification Inbox | `(Global Panel)` | All | Bell icon drop-down showing real-time alerts. Served by `GET /notifications` (own in-app notifications, `meta.unread_count`), `PATCH /notifications/{id}/read` and `POST /notifications/read-all`; the badge follows the `notifications.unread` socket event (FMS-48). Built (FMS-49): the badge, a panel of the last 30 days with mark-all-read, and a click that opens the screen the alert is about ([alerts.md](alerts.md#the-bell-fms-49)). |
| S-18 | Analytics Dashboard | `/analytics` | Admin, Fleet Owner, Fleet Mgr | KPI bands and charts for fleet health/costs (cached data). |
| S-19 | Predictive Maintenance UI | `/maintenance/ai-health` | Admin, Fleet Mgr, Tech | Shows AI health score (0-100) and top contributing risk factors. For a high-risk vehicle a technician says what the inspection found (FMS-128, [vehicle-health.md](vehicle-health.md)). |
| S-21 | Routes | `/routes` | Admin, Fleet Mgr, Dispatcher, Depot Admin (write); Compliance, Fleet Owner (read) | Planned routes trips are created from: origin, destination, ordered stops, expected distance and time (manual until FMS-84). Active and Retired views, search, depot filter. Add and edit in a side panel; retire with a confirmation (kept for past trips) and restore from Retired (FMS-83). |
| S-20 | Users and Roles | `/users` | Admin (write); Fleet Mgr, Compliance (read) | Account directory with role and status filters. Invite users (one-time setup link), change roles within your own permissions, deactivate (signs the user out at once), issue reset links, browse role permissions (FMS-78). Role controls (the Roles overview and filter, role editor) need `roles:read`; inviting and editing need `users:write`. Inviting a **driver** offers *Add driver profile* next, which opens the licence form for that account (`POST /drivers` with `user_id`) for anyone who also holds `driver:write`; a driver without a profile cannot be dispatched (FMS-79). |
| S-22 | Vehicle History | `/vehicles/{id}` | Every staff role that reads vehicles; each section with its own read permission | One vehicle's whole history (FMS-136), opened from the History link on each row of S-04. A header with plate, make and model, status, maintenance flag, health badge, depot, odometer, fuel use, when it joined the fleet and its trip under way; then one section at a time, kept in the URL (`?section=`): Repairs (every repair, service and inspection, newest first, with parts, labour, cost and odometer; a row opens the record; Log a repair with `maintenance:write`), Trips and routes (every trip with route, driver, distance, time and status; a trip opens a panel with its planned route and the GPS track it drove on a map, `tracking:read`), Fuel and odometer (fill-ups with litres, ETB and station with `fuel:read`; odometer readings with their source, a suspect one struck through), Documents (expiry badges, open, upload) and Health (the FMS-56 breakdown and Schedule inspection). A section the user may not read is not shown. |

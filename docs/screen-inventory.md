# FMS Screen Inventory

All screens must handle 5 states: Loading (Skeleton), Empty, Error (Boundary), Success, and Skeleton-on-filter.

| ID   | Name | Route | Allowed Roles | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S-01 | Login | `/login` | All | Single entry point. Displays generic auth errors. |
| S-01a | Set Password | `/set-password#token=…` | Public (one-time link) | Choose a first or new password from an invitation or reset link (FMS-78). The token stays in the fragment and is removed from the address bar. |
| S-04 | Vehicle Dashboard | `/vehicles` | Admin, Fleet Mgr, Dispatcher, Depot Admin | Data table with status badges and inline decommissioning. |
| S-05 | Driver Dashboard | `/drivers` | Admin, Fleet Mgr, Dispatcher, Depot Admin | Displays driver license status. Red 'EXPIRED' badges. |
| S-06 | Attendance UI | `/attendance` | Admin, Depot Admin, Fleet Mgr | Grid toggle (present/absent/on_leave) per driver. Each card shows the day's check-in and check-out time and how each was recorded (QR scan or by hand); on today's roster, staff who write attendance record an arrival or leaving by hand, now, for a driver whose phone has no signal. *Check-in code* opens S-24 for `attendance:qr` (FMS-141, [attendance-check-in.md](attendance-check-in.md)). |
| S-24 | Check-in code | `/attendance/qr` (`?depot=`) | Admin, Fleet Mgr, Dispatcher, Depot Admin (`attendance:qr`; never drivers) | The depot's QR for drivers to scan, for a screen at the gate: depot name, its start time and late line, the code (dark on white in both themes), a countdown to the next one, and full screen. A new code every 30 seconds, fetched on the server's schedule; a code past its life is never shown, giving way to *Reconnecting*. Someone over several depots chooses one first (FMS-141). |
| S-25 | Driver check-in | `/driver/check-in` | Driver (`attendance:check-in`) | The *Check in* tab: today's check-in and check-out times, and *Scan depot code* (*Scan to check out* once in), which opens the back camera and reads the QR with the browser's BarcodeDetector, or jsQR where there is none; any other QR in view is ignored. The result in large type (on time or late, checked out); refusals in the driver's words (expired code, another depot's, already checked in or out); a blocked or missing camera, or no signal, says to ask the supervisor. My day (S-11) shows whether the driver has checked in and leads here (FMS-141). |
| S-07 | Dispatcher Trip Console | `/dispatch` | Admin, Fleet Mgr, Dispatcher (`trip:assign`) | Split view: the depot's trips from today (Unassigned and Assigned views), and trips Under way whenever they started, on the left; its drivers and vehicles on the right with their state now (drivers green available, amber finishing a trip, red on a trip, grey absent; vehicles green available, red flagged, grey on a trip) and whether they are free for the chosen trip; search by name, plate or model. Choosing a trip, a driver and a vehicle fills the assignment panel; a flagged vehicle shows its open work order there, and the API's refusals (BR-1 and BR-3 with the conflicting trip, BR-2, eligibility) and warnings (absent driver, overrun) stay inline in it. A vehicle due or overdue for preventive service says so on its card (amber due, red overdue, with the programs) and in the panel; it can still be assigned (FMS-129). Refreshes every 30 s (FMS-33). With `trip:write` (FMS-139, docs/flexible-dispatch.md): *New trip* opens a side panel to create a trip from one of the depot's routes (a blank end takes the route's estimated time) or a one-off trip from a place to a place, times in Addis Ababa; *Change times* on the chosen trip (*Change end* for one under way) moves it, asks for a reason once it is assigned, and, when it runs into the driver's or vehicle's next trip, offers inline to move that trip later by the minutes needed. Arrival times (FMS-86, [eta.md](eta.md)): each card under way says when it arrives and how far is left, and its panel lists the stops with their times or when they were reached, says when the estimate is from a position over 2 minutes old, and moves as new estimates arrive over the socket. |
| S-30 | Backups | `/backups` (Administration menu) | Admin (`backup:read`; Back up now with `backup:write`) | Database backups (FMS-144, [backups.md](backups.md)): the last good backup, the nightly schedule and retention (30 days), and the last restore drill (or that there has been none); every backup newest first with when it was taken, nightly or Back up now, its status (queued, running, succeeded, failed with pg_dump's error, expired), size, who started it and its SHA-256; Download on a succeeded one (a signed link alive 5 minutes, audited). Back up now asks first, then the list is watched until it ends; it waits while one is under way, and says when the server has no credentials for pg_dump. Restoring is a command, never a button. |
| S-08 | Technician Repair Log | `/maintenance` | Admin, Tech | Form to log repairs, deduct stock, and clear maintenance flags. A repair records what failed (component, mode, unplanned) before it can be completed (FMS-125). A *Service due* view (`?view=service`, `vehicle:read`) lists the vehicles due or overdue under their service programs, most urgent first, filtered by status (`due`) and depot; *Log service* opens the form as preventive work under that program, and completing it starts the program's count again (FMS-129, [service-programs.md](service-programs.md)). |
| S-09 | Spare Parts Console | `/inventory` | Admin, Depot Admin, Tech | Live stock counts with 'OUT OF STOCK' / 'LOW' highlights. |
| S-10 | Audit Log UI | `/audit` | Admin, Fleet Owner, Compliance (`audit:read`) | Who changed what and when, newest first (FMS-29, [audit-log.md](audit-log.md)): views for data changes, sign-ins, denied requests, scheduled jobs and everything; find a record by its ID, filter by kind of record and period, and narrow to one person, record or request from an entry. An entry opens the fields it changed and only those, before and after, with money in ETB and fuel in litres. Read-only. |
| S-11 | Driver Mobile Home | `/driver` | Driver (`trip:execute`) | "My day" in large type: the trip under way, or the next one assigned today (times in Addis Ababa, from and to, the vehicle's plate, stops), other trips later today, and one large button: START TRIP (`POST /trips/{id}/start`), then END TRIP (`/end`), which leads to the post-trip inspection prompt (S-12). Refusals (flagged vehicle, expired licence) show under the button. Today only: no past trips. Works at 375px; with no signal it shows the day as last saved on the phone and START/END wait for signal (FMS-35). While a trip is under way the phone is the vehicle's GPS (FMS-57, [phone-gps.md](phone-gps.md)): the trip card says whether the location is being shared, the signal is weak or location is blocked, and a banner on every driver tab says when it is blocked, with Try again. |
| S-12 | Driver DVIR Form | `/driver/dvir` (`?trip=&type=`) | Driver (`dvir:write`) | "Vehicle inspection": the pre- or post-trip checklist for the vehicle of the driver's own trip (from `?trip=`, else the trip under way or the next one today). Pre-trip: tyres, brakes, lights and indicators, windscreen and mirrors, horn, fuel level, engine oil, coolant, fire extinguisher, first aid kit; post-trip: tyres, brakes, lights and indicators, body damage, leaks, dashboard warning lights, fuel level, cabin. Each item needs OK or Problem, a problem takes an optional note (the defect detail), and any problem warns that the vehicle will be flagged. Submits `POST /dvir-reports` through the offline queue (FMS-81) with `submitted_at` and the trip: with no signal it is saved on the phone with the pending badge and sent when the signal returns. Reached from the Inspection tab, the next trip's "Pre-trip inspection" link and the post-trip prompt on S-11. Submit is disabled while 50 submissions wait (FMS-37). |
| S-13 | Driver Incident Form | `/driver/incident` (`?trip=`) | Driver (`incident:write`) | "Report an incident" from wherever the driver is: what happened (accident, breakdown, tyre, theft, vandalism, injury, road hazard, other; optional), how serious (Minor, Moderate, Serious, Critical, in plain words; Critical warns that dispatch is alerted and the vehicle must not be driven), a description, and optionally the phone's location. About the vehicle of the trip under way or the next one today; with no trip it goes without a vehicle. Submits `POST /incidents` with `occurred_at` (when it was reported) through the offline queue (FMS-81): with no signal it waits on the phone with the pending badge. The Incident tab (FMS-38, docs/incidents.md). |
| S-14 | Driver Fuel Entry | `/driver/fuel` (`?trip=`) | Driver (`fuel:write`) | "Log fuel" for the vehicle of the driver's own trip (from `?trip=`, else the trip under way or the next one today): litres (`FuelInput`), total paid (`MoneyInput`, with the price per litre shown), odometer (`OdometerInput`) and an optional station. Sent as whole numbers: 23.5 L is `quantity_ml: 23500`, ETB 2,890.50 is `total_cost_cents: 289050`; a negative amount is refused in the form. Submits `POST /fuel-logs` through the offline queue (FMS-81): with no signal it waits on the phone with the pending badge. The body carries `fueled_at`, the moment the driver pressed submit, so a fill-up sent hours later keeps its pump time (FMS-115). A refusal (odometer lower than the last reading) shows the server's reason and keeps the form. The Fuel tab (FMS-40). |
| S-15 | Fuel Reconciliation | `/fuel-reconciliation` | Admin, Fleet Manager, Finance Clerk, Compliance (vehicles and anomalies); Depot Admin (anomalies only) | A month's fuel logged per vehicle against what its trips needed, furthest off first (Flag beyond the threshold in red, Watch, OK, Not judged), with a summary band, CSV export and data freshness; the fuel anomalies, resolved or dismissed with who did it recorded (FMS-42, docs/fuel-reconciliation.md). |
| S-32 | Geofences | `/geofences` (Command center menu) | Everyone with `tracking:read` (Admin, Fleet Mgr, Dispatcher, Depot Admin, Compliance, Fleet Owner); Admin draws and changes them (`geofence:write`) | The depots' yards and no-go zones (FMS-90, [geofences.md](geofences.md)): a list (name, *Stay inside* or *Keep out*, depot, *Only when not on a trip*, size) beside a map of them, the depot filter and *In force* / *Removed*. An admin draws one in a panel by clicking its corners on a map in order around the area (dragging one moves it; *Undo corner*, *Clear*), checked while drawing (3 corners, no crossing edges, 20 m across), names it, picks its depot, kind and when it applies; changes it (only what changed, with If-Match), removes it after a confirmation, and restores a removed one. Readers see the same panel without the editing. The live map (S-16) draws the depot's fences too. |
| S-16 | Fleet Command Center | `/command-center` | Admin, Fleet Mgr, Dispatcher | 70% Live Map (Socket.io/Leaflet), 30% sidebar of active trips. Counters above the map (vehicles, drivers, today's trips, what needs attention) and, beside it, the active alerts (acknowledge, resolve) and incidents (take on, status, notes in a drawer; `incident:manage`), live over the socket; a breakdown incident marks its vehicle and an at-risk vehicle's card shows its health score (FMS-89, docs/live-map.md). *Focus on driver* (or `?driver=` in the address) shows only that driver's vehicle, followed live, with the trip's planned route and today's GPS track, and a panel with driver, vehicle, trip, speed and last ping; *Whole fleet* or Esc returns to every vehicle; an unknown driver or one outside the depots shows not found (FMS-140, docs/live-map.md). A vehicle card on a trip, and the focus panel, show its arrival time and next stop, live (FMS-86, [eta.md](eta.md)). |
| S-17 | Notification Inbox | `(Global Panel)` | All | Bell icon drop-down showing real-time alerts. Served by `GET /notifications` (own in-app notifications, `meta.unread_count`), `PATCH /notifications/{id}/read` and `POST /notifications/read-all`; the badge follows the `notifications.unread` socket event (FMS-48). Built (FMS-49): the badge, a panel of the last 30 days with mark-all-read, and a click that opens the screen the alert is about ([alerts.md](alerts.md#the-bell-fms-49)). |
| S-18 | Analytics Dashboard | `/dashboard` (`/analytics` redirects) | Admin, Fleet Owner, Fleet Mgr, Dispatcher (`analytics:read`; others keep the menu home) | KPI band (utilisation, cost per km, fleet cost, on time), utilisation and cost by day, utilisation by vehicle (the whole fleet), open alerts and the vehicles most at risk; range (7, 30, 90 days, 12 months) and depot in the URL; a banner when the cache is more than 25 hours old (FMS-51, Chart.js) |
| S-19 | Predictive Maintenance UI | `/maintenance/ai-health` | Admin, Fleet Mgr, Tech | Shows AI health score (0-100) and top contributing risk factors. For a high-risk vehicle a technician says what the inspection found (FMS-128, [vehicle-health.md](vehicle-health.md)). |
| S-21 | Routes | `/routes` | Admin, Fleet Mgr, Dispatcher, Depot Admin (write); Compliance, Fleet Owner (read) | Planned routes trips are created from: origin, destination, ordered stops, expected distance and time, measured along the road when both ends are set (FMS-84), each marked "by road" or "typed in"; editing a measured route shows its road on a map. Active and Retired views, search, depot filter. Add and edit in a side panel; retire with a confirmation (kept for past trips) and restore from Retired (FMS-83). Editing a route with 2 or more stops offers *Shortest order* (`route:write`, FMS-86, [eta.md](eta.md)): the routing engine's order of the saved stops is put into the form with what it saves, to check, undo or save; it needs every stop and both ends on the map and the stops saved first. |
| S-20 | Users and Roles | `/users` | Admin (write); Fleet Mgr, Compliance (read) | Account directory with role and status filters. Invite users (one-time setup link), change roles within your own permissions, deactivate (signs the user out at once), issue reset links, browse role permissions (FMS-78). Role controls (the Roles overview and filter, role editor) need `roles:read`; inviting and editing need `users:write`. Inviting a **driver** offers *Add driver profile* next, which opens the licence form for that account (`POST /drivers` with `user_id`) for anyone who also holds `driver:write`; a driver without a profile cannot be dispatched (FMS-79). |
| S-22 | Vehicle History | `/vehicles/{id}` | Every staff role that reads vehicles; each section with its own read permission | One vehicle's whole history (FMS-136), opened from the History link on each row of S-04. A header with plate, make and model, status, maintenance flag, health badge, depot, odometer, fuel use, when it joined the fleet and its trip under way; then one section at a time, kept in the URL (`?section=`): Repairs (every repair, service and inspection, newest first, with parts, labour, cost and odometer; a row opens the record; Log a repair with `maintenance:write`), Trips and routes (every trip with route, driver, distance, time and status; a trip opens a panel with its planned route and the GPS track it drove on a map, `tracking:read`), Fuel and odometer (fill-ups with litres, ETB and station with `fuel:read`; odometer readings with their source, a suspect one struck through), Documents (expiry badges, open, upload), Health (the FMS-56 breakdown and Schedule inspection) and Service due (each service program covering the vehicle: status, what made it due, the last service and the checklist; FMS-129). A service due or overdue shows as a badge in the header that opens that section. A section the user may not read is not shown. |
| S-23 | Service Programs | `/service-programs` | Admin, Fleet Mgr, Depot Admin (write, `service-program:write`); Tech, Compliance and others with `maintenance:read` (read) | Preventive service schedules (FMS-129, [service-programs.md](service-programs.md)): each program's coverage (fleet-wide or a depot; vehicle type, fuel type, make and model), its intervals in km, days and engine hours, whichever comes first, the lead window that makes a vehicle due, its checklist, and its vehicles due and overdue. Active and Retired views; add and edit in a side panel that says what is missing before anything is sent; retire with a confirmation. A depot admin edits only their depot's programs and reads the fleet-wide ones. The header links to the repair log's Service due view with how many are due. |
| S-26 | Depots | `/depots` | Admin, Fleet Owner (`depot:write`) | The yards vehicles, drivers and staff belong to (FMS-80; FMS-20 left the screen to it): Active and Inactive views with counts, search by name, code, location or city; each depot with its code, place, capacity and time zone. Add and edit in a side panel (name and location required, code upper case and unique, coordinates as a pair, an edit sent with If-Match); deactivate with a confirmation, refused while active vehicles, drivers or accounts remain (the dialog counts them); an inactive depot opens read-only with Restore ([settings.md](settings.md)). |
| S-27 | Settings | `/settings` | Admin (`settings:write`) | The rules' thresholds, changed without code (FMS-80, [settings.md](settings.md)): Driving rules (the fleet's speed limit, pings to flag and to clear, idling minutes; depots with their own, added, edited or put back on the fleet's in a side panel) and Alerts and anomalies (fuel variance, the health score's alert line, the expiry warning's lead time, each with its range, default and who changed it last, and *Use the default*). Effective within a minute, audited. |
| S-28 | Reports | `/reports` (`?report=`, `?month=`, `?depot=`) | Admin, Fleet Mgr, Dispatcher, Finance, Compliance, Fleet Owner (`analytics:read`) | Monthly reports for management (FMS-91, [reports.md](reports.md)): Fuel, Maintenance, Cost, Trip history and Driver performance, for a month (last month by default) and the user's depots or one of them, kept in the address. A preview table with a totals row, paged, says how current the data is; *Download CSV* and *Download PDF* save the file the server builds, named for the report, month and depot. An empty month says what there was none of. |
| S-33 | Fleet availability | `/availability` (`?month=`, `?depot=`, `?view=`, `?sort=`, `?vehicle=`) | Admin, Fleet Mgr, Compliance, Fleet Owner (`analytics:read` and `maintenance:read`) | How much of a month vehicles could work (FMS-142, [availability.md](availability.md)): a month (this one by default) and the user's depots or one; availability, downtime, MTBF and MTTR; downtime by reason, largest first, failures marked; the depots side by side; every vehicle, most downtime first, sortable by each figure, with *With downtime*; a vehicle's panel lists its periods in the month with the work order or failure behind each and opens its history (S-22). *Export CSV*, in hours. Says how current the data is. |
| S-31 | System health | `/system-health` (Administration menu) | Admin (`system:read`) | How FMS itself is doing (FMS-143, [system-health.md](system-health.md)): a line on top (all systems working, what is down and why, or no samples yet); a period of 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days; each component (the API, the database, the read replica, Redis, the AI service, each provider's circuit) with its status now, last latency, a strip of the period (up, partly down, down, no data) and its uptime over 24 hours, 7 days and 30 days, or that it is not set up on the server; the period's requests, 5xx share, p95 latency and outages, with the error rate and latency charted; the outages (component, start, end or going on, length, error) and the failed scheduled jobs (job, run, attempts, error) of the period. Reads every minute. |
| S-29 | Notification settings | `/notification-settings` (from the account card) | Every signed-in user | How alerts reach the user (FMS-93, [notification-channels.md](notification-channels.md)): by severity, the channels that are on (in FMS always; critical by email, SMS and push; high by push), their email address and phone number (or how to get one added), and push for this browser: turn it on (asks the browser's permission and registers the service worker), send a test, turn it off; says when push is blocked, not set up on the server, or not supported. Push stays on after sign-out; the screen tells whether this browser's subscription is the user's. |

**Photos (docs/photos.md).** People show their photo, or their initials on a colour of their own, wherever they are listed: Users and Roles (S-20), Drivers (S-05), the header and the account card. Vehicles show theirs, or their type's icon, in the vehicle list (S-04) and the Vehicle History header (S-22). Everyone sets their own from the account card (*Your photo*); an admin sets anyone's in the user's edit panel, whoever manages drivers a driver's in the driver's edit panel, and whoever edits vehicles a vehicle's in the vehicle's edit panel. A picture is cropped (a square for people, 4:3 for vehicles) and shrunk in the browser before it is sent.

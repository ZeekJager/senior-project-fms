# Design

Calm operations console (see docs/design-system.md for tokens and components).

- Mode: Operate. Scanability and consistency outrank expression.
- Colour: indigo accent on indigo-tinted neutrals; tokens in src/index.css; light and dark via `data-theme`.
- Shape: 8px controls, 12px cards, 16px sheets. Hairline borders, minimal shadow; elevation only on floating layers.
- Type: Inter variable. Roles: title 20/24px, plate 15px semibold (tabular, slashed zero), body 14px, caption 13px, group label 12px uppercase. Nothing under 12px.
- Motion: short, quiet, reduced-motion respected.
- Avoid: gradients, glows, blur, eyebrow pills, lifting cards, coloured side stripes.

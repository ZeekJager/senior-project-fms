# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Fleet staff at Ethiopian transport depots: managers, dispatchers, technicians, finance clerks, compliance officers, owners, and drivers on phones in the cab. They use it all day to run vehicles, drivers, attendance, repairs, spare parts and fuel.

## Product Purpose
Fleet Management System: every vehicle, trip and litre of fuel in one place, with each change recorded in an audit log. Success is staff finding the right record in seconds and trusting what they see.
